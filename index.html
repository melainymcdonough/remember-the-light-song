The vocal guide is a chord-aware melodic rewrite for a warm, intimate alto/lower-mezzo delivery. Longer lines are given extra 6/8 bars, and their chord is held underneath instead of rushing the words. Hear it alone or with the ukulele, practice one section, transpose the whole melody, or export a MIDI file. This is synthesized pitch guidance, not a recorded singer. Change any phrase that does not feel natural in your voice.
